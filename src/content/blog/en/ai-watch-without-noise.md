The pace of announcements in artificial intelligence is beyond what one person can read. Trying to follow everything is the surest way to retain nothing.

## Three rules

1. **Few sources, chosen on purpose.** A handful of newsletters or blogs that explain rather than announce.
2. **A fixed slot.** One hour a week, not a permanent stream.
3. **Try rather than read.** A tool you have used for an hour is worth ten articles about it.

## What stays useful

Principles outlast products: how a model is evaluated, what it costs, where it fails. Those are the questions I write down, not version names.

This list is a starting point, not a system. It will change with use.
