Most of what we call urgent is not. Learning to tell the difference is slow work, and worth it.

## The cost of speed

Fast answers feel productive. They also push the real questions further down the road, where they become more expensive.

## Small practices

- Finish one thing before opening the next.
- Leave a draft overnight before deciding.
- Prefer a short conversation to a long thread.

## No conclusion

I do not have a system, only a direction. Writing it down is one way to stay pointed that way.
